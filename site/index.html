How it works

Three seconds, no forms.

1

Capture

Press Alt + Space from anywhere — even over other apps. A quiet pill appears, ready.

2

Parse

Type gym tomorrow at 6. Slate reads the date and time itself — no fields, no pickers.

3

Land

It drops onto your day's timeline. Drag to move it, check it off, breathe. Back to work in two seconds.

Honest about it

What Slate is not.

The wedge is focus. A few things it deliberately doesn't do — so you know before you download.

  • Not a cloud service. No sync, no account. That's the point.
  • Not another AI planner. It won't schedule for you.
  • Not on your phone yet. Desktop-first, on purpose.
  • Not a 30-app hub. One fast place, not a dispatcher.
